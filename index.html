HOW TO PLAY

Five by five. Ten poker hands.

Each row and each column is a five-card poker hand. Drag one card onto another, or click two cards to swap them. Each card contributes to exactly two hands.

Ctrl+Z undoes a swap, an applied solver grid, or a new deal. Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes it. Setups (Ctrl+S) bookmarks multiple grids in this browser tab. Closing the tab clears them.

Hand points equal rank × 100 + the values of the cards making that hand. Kickers do not add points: for example, four 8s with a 10 kicker score 832. A pair is rank 2, three of a kind is 4, a flush is 6, and a royal flush is 10. Face cards are worth 11–14.

Use the solver to search for a higher total. After the first run, Continue from session best resumes from the strongest arrangement found for these 25 cards. The session survives your swaps, but resets for a new deal or changed card set. Forever continues until you stop it. The search is heuristic and may not prove a mathematically optimal grid.

Each deal has a numeric seed shown beside YOUR GRID. Use Seed to enter that number again and recreate the same deal. Custom deals also require the same 25 selected cards.
